Background

My Background

I am a Biomedical Engineering graduate specializing in artificial intelligence for healthcare, with a focus on developing data-driven solutions for medical imaging and diagnostic systems.

My work centers on applying machine learning and deep learning techniques to tasks such as image segmentation, classification, and computer-aided diagnosis. I have led team research projects in breast cancer image analysis, where I integrated domain knowledge with model development, evaluation, and deployment to explore clinically relevant solutions.

I am particularly interested in building interpretable and scalable AI systems that can support clinical decision-making, especially in resource-constrained healthcare environments. My broader goal is to translate advanced computational methods into practical tools that enhance diagnostic accuracy, accessibility, and efficiency.

Driven by a long-term vision of innovation and impact, I aim to contribute to the advancement of intelligent healthcare technologies through research, industry collaboration, and future entrepreneurial initiatives.
